# The Lighthouse Letter

## What you found

A water-stained letter, sealed with blue wax, lies on the keeper's desk.
The handwriting is hurried.

> *If you are reading this, the light has gone out again. Do not climb the
> stairs after dark. Whatever you hear, do not answer.*

## What you know

- The keeper vanished three nights ago.
- The lamp has been lit every night since.
- Fishermen in the harbour speak of a second light, far out at sea.

### What the innkeeper says

"The keeper came in every Sunday, regular as the tide. Last Sunday he
didn't, and I've not seen the lamp go dark since."
